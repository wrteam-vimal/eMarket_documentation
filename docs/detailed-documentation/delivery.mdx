# Delivery Operations & Fleet Management

Two sidebar groups cover delivery:
- **Delivery Settings**: **Delivery Settings** (time slots) and **Delivery Boy Assignment** (auto-assign).
- **Delivery Boys**: **Add Delivery Boy**, **Delivery Boy Requests**, **Manage Delivery Boys**, **Fund Transfers**, **Delivery Boy Cash**, **Delivery Boy Policies** and **Salary**.

Delivery charges are not set here: they belong to each city's delivery zone (see [Cities and Delivery Areas](./localization.mdx#3-cities-and-delivery-areas)). Delivery-boy payouts that riders ask for are approved in [Withdrawal Requests](./customer-finance.mdx#9-withdrawal-requests-withdrawal_requests). Demo mode and the Export menu work as described in the introduction ([Demo mode](./intro.mdx#6-demo-mode-read-only-panels), [Export menu](./intro.mdx#7-export-menu-csv-excel-pdf-print-copy)).

**Money words used on this page**
- **Balance** (Wallet Balance): what the delivery boy has earned (order bonuses, return-pickup bonuses) and can be paid out.
- **Cash Received** (Cash in Hand): cash-on-delivery money the delivery boy collected from customers and has not handed over yet.
- **Salary**: a fixed payment you record for salaried riders. It is a record only and does not change Balance.

## 1. Manage Delivery Boys (`/delivery_boys`)

![Manage Delivery Boys](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-boys-manage.webp)

### Overview
The list of all delivery boys of the admin's fleet. Sidebar: **Delivery Boys → Manage Delivery Boys** (permission `delivery_boy_list`). Only **Active** delivery boys are offered orders (by hand or by auto-assign). The page has two tabs: **Delivery Boys** and **Deleted delivery boys**.

### Screen layout
- **Toolbar**: search box, **Filters** (Status: All, Active, Not-Approved, Deactive, Blocked), grid / list view switch, **Export**, **Refresh** and **Add Delivery Boy**.
- **Grid cards** (as in the screenshot): initial, name, mobile, email, address, ID chip, **Balance**, **Cash Received**, **Bonus(%)** (hover for the bonus details), **Available** (Yes / No), the status badge and the View / Edit / Delete icons.

| Column (list view) | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ID / Name / Mobile / Email / Address | The delivery boy's identity and contact details. |
| Bonus(%) | Hover to see bonus type, commission %, minimum and maximum bonus. |
| Balance | Earned money that can be paid out (see Fund Transfers). |
| Cash Received | COD cash still to be handed over (see Delivery Boy Cash). |
| Status | Registered, Active, Not-Approved, Deactive or Blocked. |
| Available | Yes when the delivery boy is available for orders in the app. |
| Actions | View, Edit and Delete. |

### Deleting a delivery boy

![Delete delivery boy dialog](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-boy-delete-preview.webp)

1. Click **Delete**. The dialog **Delete this delivery boy?** explains that the login is switched off, the delivery boy is removed from the lists and the email can be used to register again. It warns that any amount in the wallet is lost, and how many active orders are still assigned (in the screenshot: "3 active order(s) are still assigned to this delivery boy." for **Subhajit Roy**).
2. Tick **I agree to delete this delivery boy account.** (the **Delete** button stays disabled until the box is ticked) and click **Delete**.
3. Orders, cash collections and payouts are kept.

### Deleted delivery boys tab

![Deleted delivery boys](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-boys-deleted.webp)

Deleted delivery boys are listed here. There is no restore. The only action is **Delete permanently**: the dialog **Delete this delivery boy permanently?** explains that the login and uploaded documents and images are removed (from this server and external storage). Tick **I agree to delete this delivery boy permanently, with their documents and images. This cannot be undone.** and confirm. Orders, cash collections and payouts are still kept.

### Tutorial: Let a rider go without losing money or orders
Scenario: **Subhajit Roy** is leaving. He still has 3 active orders, ₹406.86 COD cash in hand and ₹615 balance.

1. Open his card with the eye icon and check **Cash in Hand**, **Wallet Balance** and **Remaining to Deliver**.
2. Reassign his open orders to other riders with **Assign Delivery Partner** on each [order's details page](./orders.mdx#2-order-details-ordersviewid).
3. Record the cash he hands over in **Delivery Boy Cash** (section 7) until **Cash Received** is 0.
4. Pay his balance with **Fund Transfers** (section 8) until **Balance** is 0.
5. Back in **Manage Delivery Boys**, click Delete, tick **I agree to delete this delivery boy account.** and click **Delete**.

**Result:** he can no longer sign in, the books are settled, and his past orders and cash records stay in the reports.

:::caution Tips & common mistakes
- Use Edit → **Status** → Deactive to pause a rider; deleting cannot be undone from the panel.
- Delete only after the cash in hand and the wallet are settled; the dialog warns that the wallet amount is lost.
- A seller's own delivery boys are managed by the seller (in the seller panel) and are not part of this admin fleet.
:::

## 3. Add / Edit Delivery Boy (`/delivery_boys/create`, `/delivery_boys/edit/:id`)

![Add Delivery Boy](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-boys-add.webp)

### Overview
The **Create Delivery Boy** form (**Delivery Boys → Add Delivery Boy**, permission `delivery_boy_create`) and the edit form (**Edit** on the list or details page). A delivery boy added by an admin is saved as **Active** and can sign in to the delivery boy app at once. **View Delivery boys** at the top right goes back to the list. With more than one active language, the **Name** can be filled per language (with **Translate Empty Fields** / **Translate & Overwrite**); the default language must be filled first.

### Field reference

| Field | Required | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Yes | Rider's name (per language). | Subhajit Roy |
| Date Of Birth | Yes | Shown on the details page and in requests. | 15 Sep 1995 |
| Mobile | Yes | Phone number. | 8715560060 |
| Email | Yes | Login email for the delivery boy app; must not belong to another panel login. | delivery9@test.com |
| Password / Confirm Password | Yes when adding | Must match. | |
| Select or Search City | Yes | One or more cities the rider serves. Auto-assign only offers orders from sellers in these cities. | Kolkata |
| Bonus Type | Yes | **Commission** (a % of each order) or **Fixed/Salaried** (no per-order bonus). | Commission |
| Bonus Percentage (%) | Yes for Commission | At least 0.1. | 5 |
| Minimum bonus amount / Maximum bonus amount | No | Limits per order; set 0 to remove a limit. | 15 / 50 |
| Custom Fields | As set per field | By default: Driving License, National ID Card, Bank Name, Bank A/C No., Bank IFSC Code, A/C Name, Address, Other Payment Info. | |
| Status (edit only) | Yes | Registered, Active, Not-Approved, Deactive, Block. | Active |
| Remark (edit only) | Yes for Not-Approved, Deactive, Block | Reason shown to the rider. | Documents expired |

How the Commission bonus is worked out: order final total × Bonus Percentage / 100, then raised to the minimum or cut to the maximum (a limit of 0 is ignored). Example: 5% of an order of ₹420 = ₹21; with minimum 15 and maximum 50 the rider gets ₹21. For an order of ₹200 (5% = ₹10) the rider gets the minimum ₹15. The bonus is fixed when the rider is assigned to the order.

**Add Default Bonus** appears when the default bonus is switched on in [Store Settings](./settings.mdx#2-store-settings-store_settings) (**Bonus Settings**); it fills in the default bonus type and amounts. On the edit page, **Reset Bonus** clears the bonus values.

### Tutorial: Add a delivery boy with bonus settings

![Bonus details with the Commission type](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-boys-add-bonus.webp)
Scenario: a new part-time rider in Kolkata is paid 5% per order, at least ₹15 and at most ₹50.

1. Open **Delivery Boys → Add Delivery Boy**.
2. Enter **Name**, **Date Of Birth**, **Mobile**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**.
3. In **Select or Search City**, pick `Kolkata`. Add a second city if the rider also covers it.
4. In **Delivery Boy Bonus Details**, choose **Bonus Type** `Commission`, enter **Bonus Percentage (%)** `5`, **Minimum bonus amount** `15` and **Maximum bonus amount** `50`. (Or click **Add Default Bonus** to copy your store defaults.)
5. Fill the **Custom Fields**: upload the **Driving License** and **National ID Card**, and enter the bank details for payouts.
6. Click **Save**.

**Result:** the rider appears in **Manage Delivery Boys** as Active with **Bonus(%)** 5, can sign in to the delivery boy app, and is offered Kolkata orders (manually or by auto-assign).

For a full-time rider, choose **Bonus Type** `Fixed/Salaried` instead and record the monthly pay in **Salary** (section 9).

:::caution Tips & common mistakes
- **Bonus Percentage** must be at least 0.1 for the Commission type; 0 is refused.
- A rider with no city, or the wrong city, is never offered orders by auto-assign.
- Changing the bonus does not change bonuses already fixed on assigned orders.
:::

![Add New Time Slot form](/img/detailed-documentation/time-slot-add.webp)

### Tutorial: Configure time slots for a grocery store
Scenario: the Grocery module delivers in four windows a day, bookable up to 7 days ahead.

1. Select **Grocery** in the panel header and open **Delivery Settings → Delivery Settings**.
2. In **Time Slot Config**, switch **Enable / Disable Time Slots** on, choose **Fixed Time Window**, enter `7` in **How many Days you want to allow?** and click **Save**.
3. Click **Add New Time Slot**. **Title** `Morning`, **From Time** 07:00, **To Time** 10:00, **Last Order Time** 06:00, **Status** Activate. Click **Save**.
4. Repeat for `Afternoon` (12:00–15:00, last order 11:00), `Evening` (16:00–19:00, last order 15:00) and `Night` (19:00–22:00, last order 18:00).
5. Check that the delivery charges for your cities are set in **Location → Cities** (see [Cities and Delivery Areas](./localization.mdx#3-cities-and-delivery-areas)); the time slots do not set prices, except for **Free Delivery** per slot.

**Result:** at checkout in the Grocery module, customers pick a day within the next 7 days and one of the four windows.

:::caution Tips & common mistakes
- Slots are per module: slots made while Pharmacy is selected do not show in Grocery.
- Switching **Slot Type** hides the slots of the other type from the table; they are not deleted.
- **Quick Delivery** is refused unless **One Seller Cart** is on.
:::

## 6. Delivery Boy Assignment (`/auto-assign-settings`)

![Delivery Boy Assignment](/img/detailed-documentation/auto-assign-settings.webp)

### Overview
Sidebar: **Delivery Settings → Delivery Boy Assignment** (permission `seller_delivery_boys`). Two cards: **Auto-Assign Settings** ("Control how new orders are offered to nearby delivery boys for the selected module") and **Seller Delivery Boys**. Settings are per module.

### How auto-assign works
1. When an order reaches **Shipped** (Quick Delivery orders already at **Received**), eMarket looks for drivers. Orders shipped with an external courier (courier name, tracking number or tracking URL filled) are skipped.
2. Eligible drivers are **Active**, serve one of the seller's cities, and belong to the right pool: the admin's fleet, or the seller's own delivery boys if the seller uses **Own Delivery Boys**. Online drivers are preferred; drivers already at **Max Concurrent Orders / Driver** are left out.
3. All eligible drivers get the offer in the delivery boy app, nearest first (distance from the store, only when the driver's location is fresher than **Location Staleness**). The first driver to accept gets the order.
4. If nobody accepts within **Response Timeout**, a new round starts without the drivers who declined or timed out, up to **Max Rounds** or **Total Wait Cap**. Then the admins are notified that the order needs manual assignment.

### Field reference

| Field | Required | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Auto-Assign Enabled | Yes | "Automatically send new orders to nearby drivers instead of assigning manually." | On |
| Response Timeout (seconds) | No | "How long a driver has to accept before it moves to the next driver." Minimum 60. | 60 |
| Max Rounds | No | "How many times to retry with drivers before giving up." Minimum 1. | 4 |
| Total Wait Cap (seconds) | No | "Maximum total time to keep trying before it gives up." | 300 |
| Max Concurrent Orders / Driver | No | "Stop offering new orders to a driver already this busy. Set 0 for unlimited." | 2 |
| Location Staleness (minutes) | No | "Ignore a driver's location if it hasn't updated in this long." | 10 |
| Allow Sellers' Own Drivers | Yes | On: sellers whose **Who Delivers Their Orders** is Own Delivery Boys use their own drivers. Off: every seller uses the admin's drivers. | On |

Each card has its own **Update** button.

### Tutorial: Turn on auto-assign and tune its rounds
Scenario: evenings are busy and dispatch by hand is too slow. You want each driver to get 60 seconds, at most 4 rounds within 5 minutes, and no driver with more than 2 open orders.

1. Select the module (for example **Grocery**) and open **Delivery Settings → Delivery Boy Assignment**.
2. Switch **Auto-Assign Enabled** on.
3. Enter **Response Timeout (seconds)** `60`, **Max Rounds** `4`, **Total Wait Cap (seconds)** `300`, **Max Concurrent Orders / Driver** `2` and **Location Staleness (minutes)** `10`.
4. Click **Update** in the Auto-Assign Settings card.
5. Make sure the riders have the right cities (section 3) and keep the delivery boy app open and online during their shift.

**Result:** when an order of this module is marked Shipped, the nearest free riders get an offer in the app. If no one accepts within 5 minutes or 4 rounds, the admins are notified to assign the order by hand.

:::warning Scheduler required
Timed-out offers are moved on by the scheduled command `auto-assign:sweep-timeouts`, which runs every minute. Without the server cron `* * * * * php artisan schedule:run`, an order whose first round is ignored is never offered again.
:::

:::tip Tips & common mistakes
- **Response Timeout** below 60 is refused.
- Riders who are offline are only used when no rider in the pool is online.
- Turning **Allow Sellers' Own Drivers** off sends every seller's orders to the admin's fleet, even for sellers set to Own Delivery Boys.
:::

## 7. Delivery Boy Cash (`/cash_collection`)

### Tutorial: Record a cash collection from a delivery boy

![Add Cash Collection form](/img/detailed-documentation/cash-collection-add.webp)

1. Open **Delivery Boys → Delivery Boy Cash** and click **Add Cash Collection**.
2. Select **Delivery Boy** `Subhajit Roy`; the form shows his cash in hand.
3. Enter **Amount** `406.86`, pick the **Date & Time** and type `Cash deposited to hub` in **Message**.
4. Click **Save**.

**Result:** a **Delivery Boy Cash Collection** row appears at the top, his **Cash Received** in Manage Delivery Boys drops to 0, and **Cash Deposited** on his details page goes up.

:::tip Tips & common mistakes
- An amount above the cash the rider holds is refused, so count the cash first.
- Use the **Delivery Boy** filter with a date range to reconcile one rider's day.
- Cash collections cannot be edited or deleted from this page; record exactly what you received.
:::

## Real-World Use Cases

- **COD cash at the end of a shift**: a rider hands over the day's cash at the hub; the cashier records it in **Delivery Boy Cash**, and the rider's **Cash Received** goes down by that amount.
- **Weekly payouts**: finance pays each rider's earned balance with **Fund Transfers** (or approves his withdrawal request), so the opening and closing balance of every payout is on record.
- **Mixed fleet**: full-time riders use the **Fixed/Salaried** bonus type and get monthly entries in **Salary**; part-time riders use **Commission** with a minimum and maximum bonus per order.
- **Busy evenings**: with **Auto-Assign Enabled**, a 60-second **Response Timeout** and **Max Concurrent Orders / Driver** set to 2, new orders go to the nearest free rider without manual dispatch.
